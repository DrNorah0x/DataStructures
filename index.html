How to use the simulators

  • Choose a program from the menu at the top. The slide numbers are shown next to each one.
  • Press Next and Back, drag the slider, or use ← → and Space on a computer.
  • Yellow always means "changed by this step" — in the code, the drawing, and the variables.
  • Some programs have a text box: type your own input and press Run it.
